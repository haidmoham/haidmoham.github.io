Working Notes

A place to inspect the work behind the work: reasoning, implementation decisions, revisions, demonstrations, notebooks, and technical writing.

How I use this space
  1. 01

    Start with a real, evaluable question.

    Write when there is an approach to test, a constraint to work through, an assumption to examine, or a result worth understanding.

  2. 02

    Keep the decision trail visible.

    Show the options considered, the tradeoffs made, the revisions that followed, and the evidence that changed the direction.

  3. 03

    Show the work when it helps.

    Use runnable demonstrations, notebooks, or implementation detail when they make a claim easier to inspect—not just to decorate it.
