the longer version

I worked on financial reporting infrastructure at Microsoft and institutional analytics at the University of Virginia. I hold a B.S. in Statistics from Virginia Tech.

At Microsoft, I worked on financial reporting systems: an SSAS Multidimensional-to-Tabular migration delivered with zero downtime, validation and operational support, automated financial-value reconciliation, and Azure-based reporting pipelines. Production telemetry and user reports showed up to 200x faster representative filtering and approximately 4x faster Excel loading.

At UVA, I owned Tableau analytics products from discovery through implementation, deployment, and support. I translated ambiguous operational questions into SQL-backed definitions and decision-support dashboards, and contributed to telemetry-informed work that improved organization-wide dashboard load times by approximately 20%.

My current robotics projects are the Robotics Test Bench and C-1N. The bench covers controlled MuJoCo experiments. C-1N is a six-legged simulation with a recorded standing baseline. Walking is next. I also build Indigo Circuit, a DuckDB/dbt analytics product.
