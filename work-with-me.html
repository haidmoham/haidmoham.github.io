Is This a Fit?

I work best when the problem is concrete and the deliverable is clear. If any of these sound like your situation, we should talk:

✓

Your data pipelines work, but they're slow, fragile, or impossible to maintain — and you need someone to rebuild them properly.

✓

You have data and need a dashboard that stakeholders will actually use, not one that gets opened once and forgotten.

✓

You're generating large volumes of text, images, or structured data and want to automate the analysis or production of that content.

✓

You need to migrate off a legacy BI or data tool and want someone who's done it before at enterprise scale — without breaking things for end users.

✓

Your Tableau environment is sluggish and you want a performance audit with clear, actionable fixes.

✓

You want to explore what AI-assisted workflows could look like for your content or reporting operations before committing to a larger build.

If any of that sounds like your situation, get in touch.
